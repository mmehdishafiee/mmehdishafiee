I'm a frontend developer and digital marketing specialist from **Shiraz, Iran**. I build products for the Persian web — where good typography, proper **RTL**, and real performance still need fighting for — and I grow them with SEO, content, and community.

- 🏢 Currently at **[leepaak](https://leepaak.ir)** — the 360° marketing agency I founded (web, SEO, brand)
- 🏹 Building **[shekargaah](https://shekargaah.ir)** — a Persian Product Hunt for Iranian software & AI tools
- 📓 Founded **[notionfa](https://notionfa.ir)** — Persian Notion templates, grown to **10,000+ users**
- 🎪 Co-founder of **Paaz** & organizer at **Asr Didani** — 12+ events, 500+ attendees
- ✍️ Writing at **[blog.mmehdi.ir](https://blog.mmehdi.ir)** and two newsletters: [Kuleposhti](https://blog.mmehdi.ir/%D8%A8%D8%B1%DA%AF%D9%87%E2%80%8C%D9%87%D8%A7/newsletter) (monthly) & [Notebook](https://mmehdishafiee.substack.com/) (weekly)
- 💬 Ask me about **React, RTL/localization, technical SEO, and growth for indie products**
